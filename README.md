# Nazmii

Mobile and systems developer. I build multiplayer games, self-hosted mobile infrastructure, and social apps — with a focus on shipping updates over the air instead of waiting on store review.

- Working with **Flutter**, **Kotlin**, **React Native**, **TypeScript**, and **Rust**
- Interested in realtime netcode, over-the-air update pipelines, and self-hosted developer tooling
- Reach me on [GitHub](https://github.com/HYPER12755) · [X](https://x.com/HYPER390961)

**[com.infected.uno](https://github.com/HYPER12755/com.infected.uno)** — Multiplayer UNO built on Kotlin with a Nakama realtime backend.

**[flutter-ota-kit](https://github.com/HYPER12755/flutter-ota-kit)** — Self-hosted Dart code-push. Patch Flutter apps over the air with no store release and no third-party cloud.
